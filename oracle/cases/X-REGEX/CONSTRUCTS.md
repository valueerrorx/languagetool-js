## Notes for F05

- Possessive/atomic: none in wave-1 scope (synthetic cases exercise them).
- Token regexps: StringMatcher compiles with `CASE_INSENSITIVE|UNICODE_CASE` unless case-sensitive (also forced case-sensitive when hasPosixCharacterClass); POS regexps and Match regexp_match/postag are flag 0; `<regexp>` rules use `UNICODE_CHARACTER_CLASS` (+ CI|UC unless case_sensitive).
- StringMatcher special-cases `\0` (no regex) and `\p{P}` (punctuationMatcher), and has fast paths that must equal Pattern.matches(); the fixtures test java.util.regex only.
- Replacement strings from `regexp_replace` are not part of the harvest; synthetic cases cover the Matcher replacement syntax.
